🔳 Generating the QR (do once per batch)

Target URL: https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20K01 (replace with your live WhatsApp Business number; increment K01 → K02… per batch). Use any QR generator that supports error correction level H (survives print scuffs), export SVG or 600+ dpi PNG, minimum print size 30 × 30 mm, always black on white with a quiet zone — never invert colours, never put the QR over a photo. Scan-test from 1 metre under shop lighting.

Why "Hi K01" works: the bot treats any message starting with "Hi" as a fresh start, and the full first message lands in your logs — so every batch, ad and partner gets its own code with zero extra tooling.
Print vendor note: order all four items from one local printer (Pothys/online like Printo work too) as a single job — 300 kits ≈ ₹2,500–3,500. Ask for matte, not gloss (gloss glare breaks QR scans under clinic tube lights). Get 10 extra of everything for partner packs and re-sends.
