1. Executive Overview

MediBook eliminates phone-queue frustration for patients and reduces front-desk workload for clinics — all through the world's most popular messaging app.

MediBook is a cloud-based, multi-tenant SaaS platform purpose-built for Indian dental clinics. It connects patients to dental providers through WhatsApp — a channel already used by over 500 million people in India — without requiring any app download, account creation, or phone calls.

Clinic administrators manage everything — doctors, schedules, appointments, patients, and analytics — through a clean, browser-based dashboard. The platform runs independently for each clinic, so data is completely isolated and private.

3. Patient Journey — WhatsApp Bot

The patient never needs to install anything. The entire experience happens through a conversation on WhatsApp.

3.1 Booking an Appointment

From the first message to a confirmed booking takes approximately 2–3 minutes.

📱 Booking Flow — Step by Step
1

Patient sends "Hi"

Patient messages the clinic's WhatsApp number. The bot instantly responds with a welcome greeting and a main menu with options: Book Appointment, My Appointments, Help.

2

Type Clinic Name (if multiple branches)

If the clinic has multiple locations, the patient types the name of their preferred branch. The bot uses fuzzy matching (Levenshtein distance) to find the right clinic even with typos or partial names. Single-branch clinics skip this step automatically. On a shared WhatsApp number, new patients also go through this step to be routed to their clinic.

3

Select Treatment / Specialty

A list of dental treatment categories is shown (e.g., General Dentistry, Root Canal Treatment, Orthodontics & Braces). Patient picks the relevant specialty. All appointments are always in-clinic. The bot also uses smart intent detection — if the patient's message contains keywords like "root canal", "toothache", or "braces", the treatment is pre-selected automatically.

4

Choose Dentist

Available dentists in the chosen specialty are listed with their qualifications and consultation fees. Patient selects their preferred dentist.

5

Select Date

The next 7 available days (with open slots) are presented as options. Days without availability are automatically excluded. The patient can also type shortcuts like "tomorrow", "Monday", or any day name and the bot resolves it to the correct date automatically.

6

Choose Time Slot

Available time slots for the selected date are shown (e.g., 10:00 AM, 10:30 AM). Fully booked slots are not displayed.

7

Reason for Visit (Chief Complaint)

Patient selects their primary reason for the visit: Pain/Emergency, Checkup/Cleaning, or Cosmetic/Other. This is saved with the appointment for the dentist's reference.

8

Enter Patient Details (First-Time Only)

New patients provide their full name, date of birth, and gender. Returning patients are recognized by phone number and skip this step.

9

Confirm Booking

A full summary is shown: Dentist name, clinic, date, time, reason for visit. Patient confirms with a single tap.

✓

Appointment Confirmed! 🎉

A unique Booking ID (e.g., MB12AB3CD) is sent to the patient. This ID is used for rescheduling, cancellation, and reference. The slot is locked instantly.

3.2 Managing Existing Appointments

📋

View Appointments

Patient types "My Appointments" to see a list of upcoming and past bookings with dates and doctor names.

🔄

Reschedule

Patient selects a booking, picks a new date and slot. Old slot is released, new slot is reserved atomically.

❌

Cancel

Patient selects a booking and confirms cancellation with a reason. The slot becomes available for others immediately.

3.3 Reminders & Follow-Ups

🔔

24-Hour Reminder

Automatic WhatsApp message sent the day before the appointment with all appointment details.

⏰

2-Hour Reminder

Final reminder sent 2 hours before the appointment. Reduces no-shows significantly.

⭐

Post-Visit Feedback

After the appointment date, the bot asks for a rating (1–5 stars) and optional comment.

3.4 Admin Notifications (WhatsApp + SMS)

📲

Instant WhatsApp Alerts to Admin

Every time an appointment is booked, cancelled, or rescheduled — whether via the WhatsApp bot or from the admin dashboard — the clinic admin receives an instant WhatsApp message on their registered notify phone. The message includes the booking ID, patient name, doctor, date, time, and clinic name. For reschedules, both old and new date/time are shown.

📱

SMS Alerts via Twilio

In addition to WhatsApp, the same booking, cancellation, and reschedule events also trigger an SMS to the admin's notify phone via Twilio. This ensures the admin is reached even if WhatsApp is unavailable or unread.

⚙️

Settings → Notifications

Admins configure their notification phone number in the dashboard under Settings > Notifications. A single number receives both WhatsApp and SMS alerts. No extra WhatsApp number or third-party account is needed — notifications are sent using the clinic's own WhatsApp Business number already connected to MediBook.

3.4 Opt-Out & Privacy

⚠️

Patients can type STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time to stop receiving all messages from the clinic. They can re-enable messages by typing START. This is fully automated and compliant with WhatsApp's messaging policies.

6. Automation & Background Tasks

MediBook runs several automated jobs in the background 24/7 — invisible to users but essential to the platform's reliability.

⏰ 11:30 PM IST · Daily

Slot Generator

Generates appointment time slots for all active doctors for the next 60 days, based on their configured weekly schedule and lunch breaks. Runs even if admin forgets to do it manually.

🔄 Every Hour

24h & 2h Reminders

Scans all confirmed appointments. Sends WhatsApp reminders to patients with appointments in ~24 hours and ~2 hours. Prevents no-shows without any admin action.

🔄 Every 6 Hours

Backup Reminder Runner

Catches any reminders that were missed due to network failures or WhatsApp API rate limits. Ensures delivery reliability for all patients.

⚡ Real-Time

WhatsApp Message Queue (BullMQ)

Incoming patient messages are pushed into a BullMQ Redis queue and processed by a dedicated worker in order. The queue handles traffic spikes gracefully, retries failed messages automatically (with backoff), and ensures each phone number's messages are processed sequentially. Rate limiting (30 msgs/min per phone, 60/min per tenant) is enforced at the webhook level before enqueuing.

⚡ Real-Time

WhatsApp Circuit Breaker

The WhatsApp sending layer uses a circuit breaker pattern. If Meta's API starts returning repeated errors, the circuit opens — stopping outbound messages temporarily and preventing cascading failures. The circuit auto-resets after a cooldown period or can be manually reset via the admin API. Authentication errors (401/403) and permission errors are excluded from the trip counter.

⚡ Real-Time · Every Booking Event

Admin Notifications (WhatsApp + SMS)

On every booking confirmation, cancellation, or reschedule, the system immediately sends a WhatsApp message and an SMS (via Twilio) to the clinic admin's configured notify phone. Uses the clinic's own WhatsApp Business number — no extra setup required. Configurable under Settings → Notifications.

🛡️

Duplicate Prevention: When multiple servers run simultaneously, a distributed locking system ensures each cron job runs exactly once — no duplicate reminders sent, no double-generated slots.

7. Security & Data Protection

MediBook is built with a security-first mindset. Patient data is protected at every layer.

🔐 Authentication

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds)
  • JWT tokens expire in 24 hours
  • 30-day refresh tokens (one-time use)
  • Token blacklist on every logout
  • Password reset via secure email link (1-hour expiry)

🏗️ Data Isolation

  • Each clinic has its own database schema
  • Zero chance of cross-clinic data leakage
  • Tenant ID validated on every API request
  • WhatsApp credentials encrypted (AES-256)
  • Bot session context encrypted at rest (AES-256) — patient booking data is never stored in plaintext in the database session store

🚦 Rate Limiting

  • Login: 20 attempts per 15 minutes
  • Forgot password: 5 requests per hour
  • Slot generation: 5 per hour
  • Analytics: 20 requests per minute
  • Webhook: 2,000 messages per minute
  • Bot: 30 messages per minute per phone number (Redis-backed, in-memory fallback)
  • Bot: 60 messages per minute per tenant
  • Bot: input length capped at 500 characters to prevent oversized payloads

🌐 Web Security Headers

  • HTTPS enforced (HSTS 1 year)
  • Content Security Policy (CSP)
  • Clickjacking protection (X-Frame-Options)
  • MIME type sniffing prevention

📝 Input Validation

  • All API inputs validated before processing
  • Phone numbers: 7–20 digit format enforced
  • UUIDs validated on all ID parameters
  • Message length limits on bot inputs

📋 Audit Trail

  • Every admin action permanently logged
  • Before/after values stored for all edits
  • IP address and browser recorded
  • Cannot be modified by any user
🔒

Patient Data Privacy: Patient WhatsApp conversations and medical history are stored only within the clinic's isolated database. No data is shared between clinics, and patients can opt-out of all messages at any time by texting STOP.

Shared WhatsApp Number — Multi-Tenant Routing

All tenants share a single Meta WhatsApp phone number. Routing to the correct clinic is handled by the global_bot_sessions table in the public schema:

  • New patient messages the number → bot asks them to type their clinic name → fuzzy match → tenant resolved → session stored in global_bot_sessions with the matched tenant_id.
  • Returning patient → their global_bot_sessions row already has a tenant_id → routed directly to that clinic without asking again.
  • Patient types "Switch Clinic" → tenant_id set to NULL, clinic selector shown again.

10. WhatsApp Conversation Flows & Message Templates

This section documents every message the bot sends — the exact text, message type, trigger condition, and conversation state — extracted directly from the source code.

10.1 WhatsApp Message Types Used

MediBook uses three native WhatsApp message types. Each is chosen based on the number of options and WhatsApp platform limits:

💬

Text Message

Plain text with *bold* and _italic_ markdown. Used for confirmations, errors, reminders, and any message with more than 3 options (numbered list fallback).

API type: text

🔘

Interactive Buttons

Up to 3 tappable reply buttons. Used for yes/no decisions, main menu, gender selection, chief complaint, and booking confirmation. Button titles are max 20 characters.

API type: interactive / button

📋

Interactive List

A scrollable dropdown with sections and rows. Used for selecting hospitals, specialties, doctors, dates, and time slots. Falls back to numbered text if WhatsApp rejects it.

API type: interactive / list

⚠️

Fallback behaviour: If an interactive message fails to send (e.g. unsupported WhatsApp version), the bot automatically falls back to a plain numbered text list — so the patient can always reply with "1", "2", "3" etc.

10.6 Automated Message Templates (System-Sent)

These messages are sent automatically by the system — not triggered by patient input. They use WhatsApp Approved Message Templates via the Meta Cloud API.

ℹ️

WhatsApp Template Messages: Unlike conversational messages, these are pre-approved templates required by Meta for business-initiated messages outside a 24-hour window. The bot uses sendTemplate() for reminders, with a plain-text fallback if the template hasn't been approved yet.

10.9 Message Audit Logging

Every inbound and outbound WhatsApp message is logged to the wa_messages table for audit and compliance purposes:

FieldDescriptionExample
phonePatient's WhatsApp number919876543210
directionin (patient→bot) or out (bot→patient)out
message_typetext / buttons / listbuttons
contentFull message text🎉 Appointment Confirmed!...
wa_message_idMeta's unique message ID for delivery trackingwamid.HBg...
created_atTimestamp2025-03-17 09:01:23 IST
ℹ️

Duplicate prevention: Inbound messages are deduplicated by wa_message_id. If Meta delivers the same webhook twice (which can happen), the second delivery is silently ignored, preventing double-processing of bookings.
