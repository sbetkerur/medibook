Executive Summary

The strategic overview: what MediBook is, what we're selling, and where we're going.

🎯 The One-Line Pitch

MediBook lets any Indian clinic run a 24/7 WhatsApp appointment bot in under 10 minutes — no app downloads, no new software, no training required for patients.

💡 The Core Insight

India has 500M+ WhatsApp users. Every patient already knows how to use it. Every clinic already has a WhatsApp number. The gap is automation — and MediBook fills it with a zero-friction SaaS that plugs into Meta's Cloud API, runs per-tenant on PostgreSQL, and costs less than a part-time receptionist per month.

Key capabilities that differentiate MediBook at launch: dental intent shortcuts (patients type "braces" or "root canal" and the bot routes them to the right specialist automatically); voice message support (patients send WhatsApp voice notes, transcribed via OpenAI Whisper); waiting list (full slots convert to future revenue instead of lost patients); post-appointment 5-star feedback collection; session resumption (incomplete bookings saved for 24 hours); real-time SSE dashboard; and a built-in bot tester so clinic demos need no Meta credentials.

🏆 Our Unique Moat — The WhatsApp-Native Advantage + Dental Intelligence

Every competitor either requires a new app, a new website, or a new workflow for patients. MediBook requires exactly zero behaviour change from patients. This is our single biggest defensible advantage and must be the centrepiece of every sales conversation.

  • Only WhatsApp booking bot with dental intent shortcuts — patients type "braces", "root canal", or "whitening" and the bot automatically routes them to the correct dental specialty. No other product does this.
  • Built-in waiting list — when slots fill up, patients join a waitlist and receive a WhatsApp notification the moment a cancellation opens. Full slots become future revenue, not lost patients.
  • Post-appointment feedback loop — after every visit, the bot automatically sends a 5-star rating request. Dentists see ratings and patient comments directly in the admin dashboard.
  • Voice message support — patients can send WhatsApp voice notes (e.g. "I need my braces tightened on Friday afternoon"); the bot transcribes via OpenAI Whisper and processes the booking. No competitor offers this.
  • Complete audit trail — every staff action (login, appointment edit, cancellation, patient record access) is logged with IP address and timestamp. Built for clinic compliance and accountability.
  • Live bot tester in the dashboard — clinic admins can test the full WhatsApp booking flow inside the browser without any Meta credentials. Demos are instant, friction-free, and impressive.
  • Session resumption — if a patient abandons a booking mid-flow, the bot saves their progress for 24 hours and offers to resume. Reduces lost bookings significantly.
  • Holiday & doctor leave management — admins mark clinic holidays and doctor leave ranges; slot generation automatically skips those dates. No double-bookings, no manual cleanup.

🎯 Channel Priority Logic

In Year 1, trust is scarce. Indian clinic owners buy from people they trust, not from ads. Hence 50% of budget goes to direct outreach and channel partners (human touchpoints).

From Month 6 onward, as testimonials accumulate, we shift 15–20% more to inbound (SEO, YouTube, referral loops) to reduce CAC.
