The strategy in one line: the QR code does every sales conversation. Physical QR kits are couriered to clinics, digital QR/click-to-WhatsApp ads run online, and every conversation after a scan happens on WhatsApp or a screen-share call. You never visit a clinic. No door-to-door. No events. No meetings.

Scripts — WhatsApp & Phone Only

Word-for-word · adapt tone, keep the structure · zero face-to-face
Field reference
P6·1 — Heads-up message (once, before the kit lands)
"Namaskara Doctor, I'm Sandeep from MediBook, Bengaluru. A small card is on its way to your reception — it's a QR code that shows, in 30 seconds, how your patients could book appointments on WhatsApp. No app, no setup. If it interests you, just scan it; if not, please ignore this message — I won't message again."

Rule: one clinic, one message. Replies only after that. This keeps the number clean and is the entire anti-spam policy.

P6·2 — Within 2 hours of a scan (they just booked a demo appointment)
"Doctor, you just booked with Dr. Sharma at Sunrise Dental 😊 — that exact 30-second flow is what your patients would get, on the WhatsApp they already use. Here's the other side: [60-sec dashboard video]. Want me to set up a free trial for your clinic? You send doctor names + timings, it's live the same day — nothing to install."
P6·3 — Follow-up cadence (stop after three)
D+1: "Any questions about what you saw yesterday, Doctor? Happy to show the clinic-side dashboard on a 10-minute screen call — 1 pm or after 8:30 pm, whichever suits."
D+3: [pilot testimonial voice note] + "This is Dr. [name] from [area] after their first month."
D+7: "Last one from me, Doctor 🙏 — the first-month-free offer closes Sunday. After that I'll assume it's a no for now and won't disturb you."
P6·4 — The objection ("my patients just call")
"They'll keep calling — this catches the bookings you never see: the 9 pm WhatsApp when the clinic is closed, and the ones your receptionist misses mid-procedure. Clinics on MediBook see a meaningful share of bookings arrive outside clinic hours. It adds a channel; it changes nothing you do today."
P6·5 — Remote close (payment)
"Sending the payment link here — ₹1,999/month, cancel anytime from your side. The moment it's paid, your booking QR and reception standee PDF come right back in this chat."

Razorpay subscription link → auto-renewal. Onboarding fee ₹2,999 applies from Month 2 signups (waived in launch offers) — you do the whole setup, they photograph their timings board.

P6·6 — Partner recruit (phone call, then post)
"You visit 15 clinics a week anyway. I'll post you 20 QR cards — when a clinic complains about front-desk call load, leave one card. If they sign up, ₹1,000 to your UPI the same week. No selling, just the card."

Cards are coded (P01…) so payouts are automatic from the scan log. Pay fast once and they hand out cards forever.
