📅 Follow-Up Sequence — After Sending the Cold Email

Day 0 — Send
Send cold email with one-pager attached
No call to action beyond "worth a conversation?" — keep it low pressure.
Day 4 — First Follow-Up
Short bump email
Subject: "Re: [original subject]" — just reply to the same thread.

Body: "Hi [Name], wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Happy to share the full technical deck if you'd like a closer look — no commitment needed. Let me know either way."
Day 10 — Value-Add Follow-Up
Add something new — don't just chase
Share a relevant stat or news item. Example: "Saw that WhatsApp Business API users in India crossed 500M active sessions last quarter — thought it added context to what I shared. Still happy to do a quick walkthrough."
Day 18 — Final Nudge
One last short message, then let go
Body: "Hi [Name], I'll keep this brief — I'm in active conversations with a couple of interested parties and expect to finalise something in the next few weeks. If MediBook was something you wanted to explore, now would be the right time. No pressure either way — happy to connect."
Day 30+ — Move On
No response after 4 touches = not interested
Move them to a low-priority list. Try a different contact at the same company, or revisit in 60 days if still unsold.

🔒 NDA Request — When They Show Interest

When a buyer asks to see the sale deck or codebase, send this before sharing anything detailed:

Subject: NDA for MediBook — [Your Name] Hi [Name], Great to hear from you. Before I share the sale deck and technical documentation, I'd like to put a standard NDA in place — this is just to protect both sides during the review process. I'll keep it simple: a one-page mutual NDA. You can use your own template if you prefer, or I'm happy to send mine. Once signed, I'll share: → Full sale deck (product, market, financials, architecture) → Technical documentation → Live demo walkthrough (I can show the bot in action) Does that work for you? [Your Name]

Equity / revenue share — Min 15% equity 20–25% equity Only consider if buyer has clear distribution (existing clinic clients)
