📅 Follow-Up Sequence — After Sending the Cold Email

Day 0 — Send
Send cold email with one-pager attached
No call to action beyond "worth a conversation?" — keep it low pressure.
Day 4 — First Follow-Up
Short bump email
Subject: "Re: [original subject]" — just reply to the same thread.

Body: "Hi [Name], wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Happy to share the full technical deck if you'd like a closer look — no commitment needed. Let me know either way."
Day 10 — Value-Add Follow-Up
Add something new — don't just chase
Share a relevant stat or news item. Example: "Thought you'd find this relevant — dental clinics using WhatsApp-based booking see 50–60% fewer no-shows in the first 30 days, simply because the reminder is in the same app patients already check constantly. Happy to share a quick demo."
Day 18 — Final Nudge
One last short message, then let go
Body: "Hi [Name], I'll keep this brief — I wanted to follow up one last time — we're currently rolling out a free 2-week trial to new clinics onboarding this month. Happy to set this up for [Clinic Name] if timing works."
Day 30+ — Move On
No response after 4 touches = not interested
Move them to a low-priority list. Try a different contact at the same clinic, or revisit in 60 days. When they do respond, always offer the 2-week free trial as the first step.

💬 Objection Handling — Common Pushbacks and How to Respond

Objection What They Mean How to Respond
"Our patients aren't tech-savvy" Fear of patient adoption "Your patients already use WhatsApp — this is literally just them sending a message. No app, no login, no form. They can even send a voice note saying 'I have a toothache' — the bot transcribes it and routes them to the right dentist. If they can send a message to a family member, they can book with you."
"We already have a receptionist" No perceived need "MediBook doesn't replace your receptionist — it frees them from 50+ calls a day so they can focus on patients in front of them. Most receptionists love it."
"₹999/month seems expensive" Price sensitivity "One missed consultation at ₹500 is ₹500 lost. MediBook prevents 15–20 no-shows a month. That's ₹7,500–₹10,000 recovered for ₹999 spent. And every new clinic starts with a free trial."
"We'll set it up later / not now" Low urgency "Totally understand. Every new clinic starts with a 2-week free trial — no credit card required. Experience the no-show reduction yourself, and upgrade only if it makes sense. Setup takes less than a day."
"What about data privacy / HIPAA?" Compliance concern "Each clinic's data sits in its own completely isolated PostgreSQL schema — your patients' records are structurally separate from every other clinic on the platform, not just access-controlled but physically partitioned. WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted AES-256. Every staff action is written to a full audit log with role-based access controls. We're hosted on Railway (SOC 2 compliant infrastructure)."
"We tried something like this before and it didn't work" Past bad experience "I'd like to understand what happened. Most failures are from tools that required a new app download or a website login — MediBook works purely on WhatsApp, which changes the adoption dynamic completely."
Golden rule: Never argue with an objection — acknowledge it first, then reframe. "I hear you — [X] is a real concern. Here's how we handle it..." always lands better than a direct counter.
