The Solution

MediBook solves it
with WhatsApp automation

Patients book, reschedule, and cancel appointments in under 3 minutes — by simply sending a WhatsApp message. No app downloads. No waiting on hold.

💬

Natural WhatsApp Bot Flow

Patients select specialty, doctor, date, and time through an intelligent conversational bot powered by Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Supports buttons, lists, and text. Async BullMQ queue for scale. Session resumes automatically if booking is interrupted — 24-hour window.

🦷

Dental Intent Shortcuts

Patients don't need to navigate menus. Type "braces" → Orthodontics auto-selected. "Root canal" → Endodontics. "Whitening" → Cosmetic Dentistry. "Emergency" or "toothache" → clinic contact shown instantly. Natural language routes patients to the right service in one message.

🏥

Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture

Each dental clinic or chain gets their own isolated schema, WhatsApp number, and bot. One platform serves hundreds of practices simultaneously with full data isolation — one clinic never sees another's patient data.

📊

Real-Time Admin Dashboard

Admin dashboard with live SSE updates across all open browser tabs — no refresh needed. Includes calendar view, waiting list management, feedback ratings tab, audit logs, and CSV export. Doctor and reception portals included.

🔔

Smart Reminders & Feedback Collection

WhatsApp reminders at 24h and 2h before appointments with department-specific pre-visit checklists. Patient confirms YES/NO — response is logged. After the visit, a 5-star feedback request is sent automatically via WhatsApp, with results visible in the dashboard.

⏳

Waiting List — No Slot Left Behind

When a patient finds no available slots, they join an automated waiting list via WhatsApp. The moment a cancellation opens a slot, they're notified instantly and can book with one reply. Full slots convert into future revenue instead of lost patients.

🎙️

Voice Message Support

Patients can send WhatsApp voice notes describing their issue — the bot transcribes them via OpenAI Whisper and processes them as booking text. No typing required. Feature-flagged per clinic, so it's enabled only where relevant.

📅

Smart Slot & Holiday Management

Doctor schedules generate time slots automatically with atomic slot locking to prevent double-booking. Nightly cron extends 60 days ahead. Admins mark holidays and doctor leave — slots are auto-blocked and no appointments are accepted on those dates.

🔐

Enterprise Security & Audit Trail

JWT auth with 30-day refresh tokens, AES-256 encryption of WhatsApp tokens at rest, Meta webhook SHA256 signature verification, per-tenant rate limiting (60 msg/min), login rate limiting (10 attempts/15 min), and complete staff audit logs — actor, IP, timestamp, and old/new values for every change.

👥

Role-Based Access & Service Catalog

Admin, staff, and doctor roles with scoped permissions. Full dental service catalog — define services with names, categories, durations, and prices. Bulk appointment updates, patient anonymisation for compliance, and bot tester built into the dashboard (no real Meta credentials needed to demo).

Security & Trust

Patient data protected
to enterprise standards

MediBook was built with clinic data security as a first-class requirement — not an afterthought. Every dental clinic's patient records are completely isolated from every other clinic on the platform.

🔒

Complete Data Isolation Per Clinic

Each clinic's data is stored in a completely isolated database schema. Your patient records never touch another clinic's data — not even in a shared table. Schema-per-tenant PostgreSQL 15 architecture.

🔑

AES-256 Encryption at Rest

WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted with AES-256 before being stored in the database. Even if the database were compromised, your WhatsApp credentials cannot be read in plain text.

📋

Complete Staff Audit Trail

Every staff action is logged — login, appointment edits, cancellations, patient record access — with actor name, IP address, timestamp, and old/new values. Know exactly who accessed patient data, when, and from which device.

🛡️

Rate Limiting & Attack Protection

Per-tenant WhatsApp rate limiting (60 messages/minute), per-phone limiting (5 messages/minute), login rate limiting (10 attempts per 15 minutes per IP), and Meta webhook SHA256 HMAC signature verification on every incoming message.
