Patients book, reschedule, and cancel appointments in under 3 minutes — by simply sending a WhatsApp message. No app downloads. No waiting on hold.

💬

Natural WhatsApp Bot Flow

Patients select specialty, doctor, date, and time through an intelligent conversational bot powered by Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Supports buttons, lists, and text. Async BullMQ queue for scale.

🏥

Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture

Each hospital or clinic gets their own isolated schema, WhatsApp number, and bot. One platform serves hundreds of practices simultaneously with full data isolation.

Multi-Portal Dashboard

WhatsApp reminders at 24h and 2h before appointments, plus booking confirmation emails via Resend with open tracking and bounce handling built in.

📅

Smart Slot Management

Doctor schedules generate time slots automatically. Atomic slot locking prevents double-booking. Nightly cron extends slots 60 days ahead with distributed Redis lock.

Enterprise Security & Ops

JWT auth, AES-256 token encryption, per-tenant rate limiting, Meta webhook signature verification, nightly pg_dump backups, and Sentry error tracking support.
