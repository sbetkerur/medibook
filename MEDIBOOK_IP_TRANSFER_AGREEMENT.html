Clause 1

Definitions

1.1

"Assigned Work" means the MediBook Marketing Strategy & Plan in its entirety, including all versions, drafts, and derivative works, as further described in Schedule A of this Agreement.

1.2

"Intellectual Property Rights" means all copyright, moral rights (to the fullest extent waivable), trade secrets, know-how, trademarks (to the extent embedded in the Assigned Work), database rights, and all other proprietary rights of any nature, whether registered or unregistered, subsisting anywhere in the world.

1.3

"Transferor" means Party A identified above, the original author and owner of all Intellectual Property Rights in the Assigned Work.

1.4

"Transferee" means Party B identified above, who acquires full ownership of all Intellectual Property Rights in the Assigned Work under this Agreement.

1.5

"Consideration" means the agreed lump-sum payment made by the Transferee to the Transferor in exchange for the assignment of rights as set out in Clause 4.

Clause 2

Assignment of Intellectual Property Rights

2.1

Full Assignment. In consideration of the payment described in Clause 4, the Transferor hereby irrevocably, unconditionally, and absolutely assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Transferee, its successors and assigns, all right, title, and interest — including all Intellectual Property Rights — in and to the Assigned Work throughout the universe in perpetuity.

2.2

Scope of Rights Assigned. The rights assigned under Clause 2.1 include, without limitation, the exclusive rights to:

  • Reproduce, copy, and distribute the Assigned Work in any form or medium
  • Modify, adapt, translate, and create derivative works based on the Assigned Work
  • Publish, broadcast, publicly display, and communicate the Assigned Work to the public
  • Sub-license, sell, transfer, or assign the Assigned Work or any rights therein to third parties
  • Commercially exploit the Assigned Work in any manner, in any market worldwide
  • Register copyright or any other IP right in the Assigned Work in any jurisdiction
  • Bring claims and recover damages for past, present, and future infringement of rights in the Assigned Work
2.3

Moral Rights Waiver. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Transferor hereby irrevocably and unconditionally waives all moral rights (including rights of attribution and integrity) in the Assigned Work in favour of the Transferee and its successors.

2.4

Further Assurances. The Transferor agrees to execute any additional documents and take any further steps reasonably requested by the Transferee to perfect, record, or enforce the assignment of rights granted herein, including filings with the Copyright Office or any other relevant authority.

2.5

No Retained Rights. Upon execution and payment, the Transferor retains no right, title, or interest of any nature in the Assigned Work and shall not use, reproduce, or exploit the Assigned Work in any manner without the prior written consent of the Transferee.

3.3

Destruction of Copies. Upon delivery, the Transferor shall permanently delete or destroy all copies of the Assigned Work in its possession or under its control, except as required by applicable law, and shall confirm the same in writing to the Transferee.

6.1
