WhatsApp-native appointment booking SaaS for Indian hospitals and clinics — fully built, production-tested, and ready to deploy on day one. Complete IP transfer · Multi-tenant · Full source code included.

What MediBook Does
  • 💬 Patients book appointments by chatting on WhatsApp — no app download, no forms, no hold music. Powered by Meta WhatsApp Cloud API with interactive buttons and list pickers.
  • 🏥 Each hospital or clinic gets their own WhatsApp bot, admin dashboard, and isolated PostgreSQL schema. True multi-tenant SaaS — one platform serving hundreds of practices simultaneously.
  • 🔔 Automated appointment reminders via WhatsApp at 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit, plus booking confirmation emails via Resend — reducing no-shows without any staff effort.
  • 📊 Clinic admins manage doctors, schedules, appointments, and patients via a Next.js dashboard with real-time SSE updates, analytics, CSV export, and slot generation.
  • 👨‍⚕️ Dedicated portals for doctors (today's schedule) and reception staff (check-in), plus a service catalog and holiday management — ready to deploy across any medical specialty.
