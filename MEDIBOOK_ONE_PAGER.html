WhatsApp-native appointment booking SaaS for Indian dental clinics — no app downloads, no missed calls, no no-shows. Get your clinic live on WhatsApp in under 24 hours. Free trial included.

What MediBook Does
  • 💬 Patients book appointments by chatting on WhatsApp — no app download, no forms, no hold music. Dental intent detection lets patients type "root canal", "braces", or "whitening" and the bot auto-selects the right specialty. Voice messages are transcribed via OpenAI Whisper and processed as text. Natural date shortcuts ("tomorrow", "Monday") are resolved automatically.
  • 🦷 Each dental clinic or chain gets their own WhatsApp bot, admin dashboard, and isolated PostgreSQL schema. True multi-tenant SaaS — one platform serving hundreds of dental practices simultaneously. Incomplete bookings are saved and resumed within 24 hours; when slots are full, patients join a waitlist and are notified via WhatsApp when a slot opens.
  • 🔔 Automated WhatsApp reminders at 24 hours and 2 hours before every visit, with department-specific pre-visit checklists. Bot tracks patient YES/NO confirmation replies. Post-appointment 5-star rating and comment collected automatically via WhatsApp — no staff effort required.
  • 📊 Clinic admins manage doctors, schedules, appointments, and patients via a Next.js dashboard with real-time SSE live updates, analytics (no-show rates, revenue per dentist, average feedback rating), CSV export, bulk actions, and calendar view.
  • 👨‍⚕️ Dedicated portals for dentists (today's schedule) and reception staff (check-in). Dental service catalog with custom pricing (cleanings, root canals, implants, braces). Holiday and doctor leave management auto-blocks affected slots. Role-based access (admin/staff/doctor) with full audit logs.
